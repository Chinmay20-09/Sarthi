The capital of Italy is Rome. Rome is located in the central-western portion of the Italian Peninsula, along the shores of the Tiber River. It is the country's most populous city and the seat of the Italian government. Rome is famous for its ancient monuments, including the Colosseum, the Roman Forum, and the Pantheon, as well as its Vatican City, home to the Pope and the headquarters of the Roman Catholic Church. The city is also known for its art, culture, and cuisine, with a rich history spanning over 2,700 years.
